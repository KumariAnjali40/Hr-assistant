LEAVE POLICY (Version 3.0)

1. Casual Leave
Every employee is entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and lapses on 31 December. A maximum of 3 consecutive days of casual leave may be taken at one time.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

3. Earned (Privilege) Leave
Earned leave accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service, i.e. 18 days per year. Up to 30 days of earned leave may be carried forward. Earned leave beyond 30 days is encashed in January. Earned leave requires approval from the reporting manager at least 7 days in advance, except in emergencies.

4. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth of the child.

5. Applying for leave
Leave must be applied through the HR portal. Leave not approved in advance may be treated as loss of pay.
