COMPENSATION REVIEW GUIDELINES FOR MANAGERS (Version 1.0) - CONFIDENTIAL

1. Review cycle
The annual compensation review runs in April. Managers must submit team recommendations by 15 March.

2. Merit increase budget
The merit increase budget is 8 to 10 percent of the team's total base salary. Increases above 15 percent for an individual require VP approval.

3. Calibration
All ratings are calibrated across the department before increases are finalised. Managers must not communicate outcomes to employees before the official announcement.
