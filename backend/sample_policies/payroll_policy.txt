PAYROLL POLICY (Version 2.1)

1. Salary credit date
Salaries are credited on the last working day of every month. If the last day is a bank holiday, salary is credited on the preceding working day.

2. Payslips
Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 3rd of the following month.

3. Reimbursements
Reimbursement claims must be submitted with receipts by the 25th of the month to be processed with that month's payroll. Claims submitted later are processed in the next cycle.

4. Tax declarations
Investment declarations for tax savings must be submitted by 31 January. Proofs must be uploaded by 15 March.

5. Salary queries
For any discrepancy in salary, raise an HR ticket within 30 days of the payslip date.
